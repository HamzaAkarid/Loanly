Built for clients and admins

Everyone gets the view they need, with the math done for them.

For clients

  • Financial overview: total debt, monthly income, expenses and remaining balance at a glance.
  • Loan and lender tracking: lenders, amounts, interest rates and live status.
  • Income and expense log: cash flow calculated automatically.
  • Calculator and application: estimate repayments, apply and track your request.

For admins

  • Application queue: requests with applicant income and existing debt.
  • One-click decisions: approve or reject and the client's view updates.
  • Portfolio overview: total requests, approved volume and active borrowers.
