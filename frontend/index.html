How it works

From first contact to the last payment, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Add a client

    Register the borrower with their contact details and basic information.

  2. 2

    Create the loan

    Set the amount, interest rate and term. The payment schedule is generated for you.

  3. 3

    Track payments

    Record each payment as it comes in and see the remaining balance update.

  4. 4

    Close the loan

    Once the balance hits zero, the loan is marked as paid and archived.
